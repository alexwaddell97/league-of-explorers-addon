# League of Explorers changelog

## 0.1.0

The first beta.

- Exploration: every zone and its areas, from the game's own map data.
- Bestiary of open-world rares, by zone and creature type.
- A weekly hunt for each level band.
- Points, ranks, seasons and leaderboards. Alts count with their main.
- Guild sharing: finds, rare alerts, expeditions and bounties from officers,
  and places members suggest.
- Map and minimap pins for rares you're hunting.
- Solo mode, for playing without a guild.
